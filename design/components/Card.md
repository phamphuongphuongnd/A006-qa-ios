# Card

`surface`, `radius-card` (16px), `shadow-card`, 16px padding (20px for hero cards).

- **Summary card**: label/value grid — label in `caption` `ink-muted`, value in `amount-md`. In = `income-text`, Out = `expense-text`, Net and miles = `ink`.
- **To sort card**: `sunday-soft` background, no shadow; the only yellow surface on Home.
- **Warning card** (`warn-soft`) and **error card** (`error-soft`) — see AlertCard.
- Cards grow vertically at 200% text; never fix their height.
