# Segmented

Two or three mutually exclusive options on a `surface-sunk` track.

- Expense | Income: selected side takes its soft fill, 1.5px ring and text colour (`expense-*` / `income-*`).
- Work | Personal: selected side is filled `work` / `personal` with `on-scope` text. `lg` (48px) on Sort cards.
- Neutral options (merge-stop minutes, SA103 | MTD categories, Standard | Calendar): selected = `surface` + `shadow-card`.
