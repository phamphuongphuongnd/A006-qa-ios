# Snackbar

Inverted (`ink` on `bg`) bar above the tab bar. Every save, delete, merge, split, swipe or auto-sort gets one with **Undo** in `sunday` for 5 seconds; plain notices have no action.
Copy from the spec: "Saved · £12.50 expense", "Saved · Fuel (auto-sorted)", "Sorted as Fuel", "Marked as personal", "Entry deleted", "Couldn't save the photo. Your entry was saved without it.", "Reminders are off. You can turn them on in Settings."
