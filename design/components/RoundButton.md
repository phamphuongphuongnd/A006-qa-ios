# RoundButton

56px circular buttons for the Quick capture row (Photo · Work/Personal · Note) and the FAB.

- Rest: `surface` with a 1.5px `line-strong` ring. Active (photo taken): `primary` fill. Work: `work` fill; Personal: `personal` fill, both with `on-scope` icon.
- Locked (free photos used up): small lock badge bottom-right; tapping opens the photo-limit sheet, never blocks saving.
- FAB: 56px, `radius-card` corners, `shadow-raised`, bottom-right on Entries, Sort, Miles, Reports. Opens Quick capture as Expense.
