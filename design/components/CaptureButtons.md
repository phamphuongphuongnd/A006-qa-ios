# CaptureButtons

The two big Home buttons, 72px tall (`tap-hero`), side by side, always in the lower half of Home.

- Expense: `expense-soft` fill, `expense-text` label, solid `expense` disc with a minus. Income: the `income` trio with a plus.
- The sign, the word and the colour all say the same thing — never rely on colour alone.
- Tapping opens Quick capture with that type selected. Other tabs use the FAB instead.
