Sunday Sort privacy policy

Sunday Sort: Driver Expenses · Effective 12 November 2026

In short: Sunday Sort has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your phone. We can't see your data, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

The entries you log (amounts, dates, notes, categories, Work/Personal), receipt photos you take, trips and mileage, and your settings. All of it is kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, does not connect to the internet, and contains no advertising, tracking or analytics code. We do not sell, share or receive any personal data.

Permissions and why

  • Location – only while a trip you started is recording, to measure the distance you drive. On Android, "Allow all the time" lets a running trip keep recording when the screen is off. Nothing is recorded when no trip is running. Your route stays on your phone.
  • Camera / photos – only when you add a receipt photo to an entry.
  • Notifications – local reminders you turn on (weekly sort, backup, trip running). They are created on your phone; there are no push notifications.
  • Face ID / fingerprint – only if you turn on App lock. The check is done by your phone's system; the app never sees your biometric data.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export (CSV, PDF, ZIP of photos), the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes – for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, email or your accountant. That service's own privacy policy then applies to the copy you send. Sunday Sort does not upload anything by itself.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected – delete them where you saved them.

Children

Sunday Sort is a record-keeping tool for self-employed drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
