Sunday Sort privacy policy

Sunday Sort: Driver Expenses · Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Sunday Sort has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. What you record is kept on your phone and is never sent to us. A few features use outside services (maps, address search, the app store for Pro, and backups you or your phone's system make) — listed below.

What the app stores

The entries you log (amounts, dates, notes, categories, Work/Personal), receipt photos you take, trips and mileage, and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Receipt reading (Pro) runs on the phone; photos are not uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and contains no advertising, tracking or analytics code. We do not sell, share or receive any personal data.

Outside services the app uses

  • Map tiles (OpenFreeMap) – when you look at a trip map, the map pictures for that area are downloaded. The recorded route itself is not sent.
  • Address search and A → B distance (openrouteservice) – only when you type or pick a place for a trip: the text you type, the places you pick, and your current position if you tap "Current location".
  • Google Play / App Store – if you start the Pro trial or subscribe, the store handles the purchase under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.
  • System backup – your phone's own backup (Android backup to your Google account, iCloud Backup on iPhone) may include the app's data, as for most apps. It is controlled in your phone's settings, not by us.

Permissions and why

  • Location – only while a trip you started is recording, to measure the distance you drive. On Android, "Allow all the time" lets a running trip keep recording when the screen is off. Nothing is recorded when no trip is running. With Automatic trips (Pro) the phone's motion detection tells the app when you start driving. Your route is never sent to us.
  • Camera / photos – only when you add a receipt photo to an entry.
  • Notifications – local reminders you turn on (weekly sort, backup, trip running). They are created on your phone; there are no push notifications.
  • Face ID / fingerprint – only if you turn on App lock. The check is done by your phone's system; the app never sees your biometric data.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export (CSV, PDF, ZIP of photos), the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes – for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, email or your accountant. That service's own privacy policy then applies to the copy you send. If you turn on nightly backup, the app saves a backup file every night to the folder you chose (for example in Google Drive) and nowhere else.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected – delete them where you saved them.

Children

Sunday Sort is a record-keeping tool for self-employed drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
